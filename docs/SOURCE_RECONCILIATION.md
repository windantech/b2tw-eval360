# B2THEWORLD — what the three documents say, and where they disagree

Read before building anything. Three source documents were supplied:

| # | Document | What it is | Numbers in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | B2THEWORLD M&E Plan Template (2024) | The narrative plan — institution, gaps, beneficiaries, strategies, data sources, sub-plans | Objectives in §2.6; a data-collection plan in §5.2 |
| **B** | B2THEWORLD GENERAL M&E LogFrame FINAL | Three projects, each a full logframe: Impact → Outcomes → Outputs → Activities → Inputs | The operational targets |
| **C** | B2THEWORLD Impact Builder POOA v5 | A **newer theory of change** — problems P1–P3, outcomes OC1–OC3, outputs OP1–OP3, assumptions AS1–AS6 | **None.** No targets, no dates, no countries |

The supplied file states plainly: *"These are source materials, not a reconciled or updated project specification. Keep older and newer plans distinct; do not invent values for blanks or resolve differing targets without user approval."*

That instruction is followed here and in the build. **Nothing below is resolved.** The system holds both plan generations side by side and shows the differences; it does not pick a winner.

## 1 · Nine places the documents disagree

### 1.1 How many students

| Source | Says |
|---|---|
| A §2.6 | "Serve **10,000 students** by May 30, 2025, in the four partner countries." |
| B row 1 | "...to a total of **6,896 students**" |
| B §6.1 Objective | "Serve **6896 children** in four partner countries" |
| B §6.1 **Output 1** | "**6898 children** receive transformative education" |
| B key activities | "Output 1: **6898 students** receive Transformative Education" |
| A §5.2 | "Serve **6896 children**" |

**Three different figures — 10,000 / 6,896 / 6,898 — and the 2-student gap between the objective and its own output sits inside a single document.**

### 1.2 Which day the year ends

**May 30, 2025** (A §2.6; B §6.1 objective) against **May 31, 2025** (B row 1, B Project 2, B Project 3). Trivial, but it changes a reporting deadline.

### 1.3 How many countries, and which

| Source | Says |
|---|---|
| A §2.3 | Rwanda, Cameroon, South Sudan, Pakistan — **4** |
| A §2.6 | "Add **two new** partner countries to the existing four" |
| B row 1 | "South Sudan, Cameroon, Pakistan, Rwanda, **and Iraq**" — **5** |
| B row 28 | "**3 current partnership countries**" |
| B row 32 | "...children impacted by war in **Iraq, South Sudan, and Cameroon**" — a different 3, with Rwanda and Pakistan absent |
| B Output 3 | Candidates: **Afghanistan, Rwanda Expansion, Iraq, DRC, CAR** — five named for two places |
| B activities | "Visit **DRC, Pakistan, Iraq and CAR**" — Pakistan is listed as current elsewhere |

**Iraq appears as a current partner, as a candidate to move in, and as a country to visit.**

### 1.4 How many schools

| Source | Says |
|---|---|
| A §2.2 | Rwanda 1+1, South Sudan 2+1, Cameroon 2+0, Pakistan 3+3 = **13** |
| A §2.6 | "Partner with **5 more schools** in three partner countries" |
| B Output 2 | "Partner with **27 schools**" — 14 primary (Rwanda 1, South Sudan 7, Cameroon 6) + 13 secondary (Rwanda 1, South Sudan 6, Cameroon 1, Pakistan 5) |

The 27 adds up correctly. But Output 2 gives Pakistan **0 primary schools** where A §2.2 gives it **3**, and 5 secondary where A gives 3. And 13 + 5 more ≠ 27.

### 1.5 How many people to train

**150** (A §2.6: "100 teachers and 50 educational leaders", in *three* countries) · **250** (B rows 28 and 38, "250 stakeholders", 3 countries) · **500** (A §5.2: "Train 500 stakeholders — teachers, school leaders, steering committee members").

### 1.6 What the goal is

- **A §2.5** lists six goals: infrastructure, number of schools, K–12 enrolment and graduation, university enrolment, teacher capacity, leader capacity.
- **B §6.1 Goal** is one sentence about attendance and literacy: *95% daily attendance; 50% change from baseline in students who can read and write.*
- **C Impact** is a third framing: continuity in education, well-being, resilience, opportunity — no numbers.

These are not versions of one goal. They are three different goals.

### 1.7 Two generations of strategy, one project

**A and B** are built on **Why / Play / Lead** and three projects: serving children, stakeholder capacity, team culture.

**C (POOA v5)** reframes everything around three problems — **teaching quality, leadership quality, school quality** — leading to a **Fullness of Life Framework** with an **Entry → Advanced** progression and a **School Self-Study** assessment. *Why/Play/Lead* survives only inside OC1.

C is clearly the later thinking. It carries no targets, so it cannot run a reporting system on its own; A and B carry the targets but not the current strategy.

### 1.8 Money appears in only one place

**$1.1M total revenue** and **$50,000 per partner country** appear in A §5.2 — the data-collection plan — and in no logframe. There is no budget line, no cost column filled in, and no financial indicator at any results level.

### 1.9 An empty outcome

B row 9 is numbered "**4**" with nothing beside it. Project 1 has three outcomes and a slot for a fourth.

## 2a · Decision taken — 12 October 2026

**Decided by the client. Where two or more documents state a different figure for the same thing,
the system uses the highest stated figure.** This is the approval the source material required.
It is recorded here, shown on the Plan Reconciliation screen with this date, and can be changed
there at any time.

| # | Conflict | Values found | **Resolved to** | Source of the winning figure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Students served | 10,000 · 6,896 · 6,898 | **10,000** | A §2.6 |
| 1.2 | Year end | 30 May 2025 · 31 May 2025 | **31 May 2025** | B rows 1, 28, 56 |
| 1.3 | Countries | 3 · 4 · 5 | **5** — Rwanda, South Sudan, Cameroon, Pakistan, Iraq | B row 1 |
| 1.4 | Partner schools | 13 · "5 more" · 27 | **27** | B Output 2 |
| 1.5 | Stakeholders trained | 150 · 250 · 500 | **500** | A §5.2 |

### Three places "highest" does not decide anything

- **§1.6 — the goal.** A, B and C state three *different* goals, not three sizes of one. All three
  are kept, each against its own source. Where a goal carries a figure, the most demanding is used:
  95% daily attendance, 50% change from baseline in reading and writing (B §6.1).
- **§1.7 — two strategy generations.** Not a number. POOA v5 remains the adopted theory of change;
  the 2024 logframe supplies the targets, because POOA v5 has none.
- **§1.9 and all of §2 — the blanks.** A blank is not a contradiction. Nothing blank is filled.
  Every gap in §2 below stays a gap with its question attached.

### One figure that needed a second ruling

Conflict §1.4 resolves to **27** at the headline, but the per-country breakdowns disagree beneath
it. Taking the highest value *cell by cell* — Pakistan's 3 primary schools from A §2.2 against
Output 2's 0 — produces **30**, which no document states.

The system uses **27 with Output 2's breakdown**, because 27 is a figure a document actually
states and 30 would be one this system invented. The 3 Pakistani primary schools in A §2.2 that
Output 2 does not account for are carried as an **open question**, not silently dropped.

### What this costs, so nobody is surprised later

Taking the highest of each figure makes every target the most ambitious one available: 10,000
students rather than 6,896, 500 stakeholders rather than 150. Achievement against plan will read
**lower** than it would against the other documents — the same delivery, measured against a
longer ruler. That is a defensible way to set targets, but when the annual report shows a
percentage, it is this choice that set the denominator. The Plan Reconciliation screen keeps the
other figures visible beside it for exactly that reason.

## 3 · How the build handles all of this

1. **Both plans are loaded, separately.** The 2024 logframe (A + B) runs the operational modules because it is the only one with targets. POOA v5 (C) is loaded as the adopted theory of change, with its own screen.
2. **A Plan Reconciliation screen** lists every difference above, quotes both sources, shows the decision of 12 October 2026 and who made it, and keeps the figures that were not chosen visible beside it. The decision can be changed there; the code never chooses on its own.
3. **Nothing blank is filled.** A blank renders as a gap with the question attached, and blocks the reports that need it — the same way a missing variance explanation blocks the FPC quarterly report.
4. **A mapping screen** shows how the 2024 outputs correspond to POOA v5's OP1–OP3, so a reader can see what carried forward and what did not.

> **One assumption, stated rather than buried:** the operational modules run on the **2024 logframe**, because POOA v5 has no targets to run on. If B2THEWORLD has since set targets against OC1–OC3, send them and the system moves over.

## 4 · Sensitive material in these documents

- **Children affected by armed conflict, persecution and displacement.** Student records are handled the way FPC's case records are: aggregates by default, no child named, small counts suppressed.
- **Project 3 is explicitly Christian** — prayer, Bible study, *Lead with Prayer*, a rule of life, the ECO App, evangelism and discipleship expeditions. It is one of three M&E projects and is built as written. It is not neutralised, and not foregrounded beyond its place.
- **Staff named in A §5.2**: Victor, Joseph, Ashraf, Benjamin, Douglas, Dr Ben, Joan. Used as role-holders because that is what the document makes them. Say the word and they become role titles.
